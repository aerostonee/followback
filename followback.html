Getting your data from Instagram

  1. Open Instagram → Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions
  2. Tap Download your information → Download or transfer information
  3. Select your account, choose Some of your information, and pick Followers and following
  4. Set the format to JSON, then request the download
  5. Once it's ready, unzip it and upload followers_1.json and following.json here
